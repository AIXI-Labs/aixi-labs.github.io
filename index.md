## Our Values

<ul class="value-list">
  <li><strong>Rigour</strong> — We prioritize high-quality, methodical research grounded in formal models, and embrace negative results when they identify flaws in safety proposals, whether our own or others'.</li>
  <li><strong>Transparency</strong> — We share our work openly where possible, including failures and dead ends.</li>
  <li><strong>Openness</strong> — We remain open to exploring new ideas and abandoning or revisiting old ones.</li>
  <li><strong>Collaboration</strong> — We believe progress requires coordination across the field.</li>
  <li><strong>Impact</strong> — We focus on research that meaningfully reduces risk. To develop results that are robust instead of superficial, we value foundational work whose payoffs may not be immediate.</li>
</ul>
